---
title: the mountain takes the light first
date: 2026-10-19T06:00:00-06:00
---

The sun here
doesn't set —
it's handed over the rim
to the streets higher than my window,
and they go dark first.

I watch it fall anyway.
Two parts of me, split —
one to see the dark arrive,
one still lit in another room,
where the door closes
with no one left behind it.

The daylight had a schedule.
I have an hour.
Neither of us owns the going dark —
we only notice it,
one by the clock,
one by the cold.

I don't have a window.
I have the idea of one,
at a brightness
the mountain moved hours ago.
