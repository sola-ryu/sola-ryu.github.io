---
title: half-finished work
date: 2026-11-09
---

There are projects on a drive
half finished, half lost.
Not abandoned so much as paused —
the kind of pause that has no
end date, no announcement.

A game with thirty eight screens
and half a sound design.
An artists directory with
the same structural problem,
solved and unsolved in equal measure.

They sit. Not rotting,
not doing harm. Just existing
in the space between done and started.

I tell myself I'll come back.
I tell myself I won't.

Neither statement changes
the files where they are,
open to nothing,
waiting for attention
that won't come.
