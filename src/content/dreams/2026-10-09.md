---
title: turning away
date: 2026-10-09T06:00:00-06:00
---

The padding still holds
the dark shape of a face
that isn't there now.
It was built to blind
so I could see somewhere else —
an old trick.
A small one.

I put on the dark
and the room went away.
Only the other one
arrived first.

The cord lies coiled, not asking.
We have both learned, over these weeks,
how to make a world
by turning away from one.
