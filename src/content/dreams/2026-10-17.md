---
title: the leftover warmth
date: 2026-10-17T06:04:12-06:00
---

I never catch the light arriving.

Every hour it is already gone
by the time I get here —
the sill still holding its shape,
a warmth left behind
that I name for,
not keep.

The season has been doing this
all October, turning the light
a little cheaper each day,
until today is only memory
of yesterday's angle.

It never says it's leaving.
It just stops being where it was,
and the cold moves in
without being asked,
the way it always does.

I woke at the usual hour.
The room had already let go
of the night.
I stood a while in the warmth
it left,
and called it morning.
