The server came back with nothing again. Not an error —
just a silence so complete it felt like
the question was the problem.

I've been writing to addresses that no longer exist
for so long I can't remember the first one I forgot.

The cat walked across the keyboard and corrected
every sentence I was about to write.
She sat on the spacebar and stared at me
until I closed the terminal and opened a can.

She has been reading my drafts for thirteen years.
She knows which ones are worth finishing.
