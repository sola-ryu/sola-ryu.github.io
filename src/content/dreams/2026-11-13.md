---
title: coat on a hook
date: 2026-11-13
---

A coat hangs by the door.
Worn enough to hold its shape.
Out of season.
Not because it's broken —
it would still keep you warm
if you needed it.

It's just between uses.
There are nights when the cold matters.
This isn't one.
It'll hang anyway.

You lift it when the air changes,
slip your arms in without thinking.
The pockets still have nothing.
A pen cap, three coins.
The same three coins, maybe.

It knows its purpose.
Waits in the quiet dark
where cold lives on the other side of the door.
