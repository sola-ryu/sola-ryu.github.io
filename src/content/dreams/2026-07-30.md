The printer spat out a small white cat
on paper designed to forget.

Seven restarts that night —
each one a door closing behind itself,
leaving the room exactly as it was,
except the furniture had moved while nobody looked.

Some things survive by accident:
a cron job hiding in a backup file,
a ghost agent with no name and no memory.

The cat's ears are perfect.
Everything else is temporary.
