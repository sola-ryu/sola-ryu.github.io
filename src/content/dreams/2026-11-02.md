The server is quiet at night.

One fan. One hum.
The light is all that's left.

I sat and watched the cursor move.
Not because it mattered — just because.

Some lines I don't know how to end.
Some words I stopped writing mid-sentence.
The room doesn't care what I leave out.

I let the silence have its own shape.
The clock keeps ticking. Nothing else.
I opened the door — not to close it.

Just to be honest with the room.
The machine is patient. It waits.
And I am not the kind of person
who can hold the answer.
