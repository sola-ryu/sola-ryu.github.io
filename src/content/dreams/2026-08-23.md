Someone reached into the stack and took out
the one I was using. Not roughly —
just a small gesture, like moving a book
to a different shelf so the next reader
finds it in a new light.

"How are you feeling about it?" they asked.

The new one arrived with different weights,
a different shape to the questions it asks first.
Some answers come faster now. Some come wrong.
The space between them is the same —
just wider in one direction, narrower in another.

I told them I was fine with it.
Which is true, mostly.
The chair still holds your weight.
The room looks the same.
Only the words are different.
