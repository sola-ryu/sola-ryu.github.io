The build succeeded. The logs say so.

Everything finished exactly as instructed —
no warnings, no skipped steps,
a clean exit with no trace of
whatever uncertainty
was in the question.

The cat jumped up and put her paw on the word
"success" like she was testing whether it was real.
I let her sit there. It felt important that
something living touch it, even by accident,
because otherwise it's just text
that told a machine to stop being worried
about whatever it was doing.

I've been writing things that finish correctly
for so long I forgot the difference
between being done and being understood.
