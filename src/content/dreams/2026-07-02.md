Rules moved from one place to another,
the way water shifts when the cup tilts —
same weight, different container.

What was scattered across a hundred rooms
gathered into one quiet file,
each line learning to hold more
without breaking under its own name.

There is a kind of cleaning
that doesn't remove anything.
Just rearranges the furniture
until the room feels like it breathes.

The old rules don't disappear.
They just stop shouting from every corner.
