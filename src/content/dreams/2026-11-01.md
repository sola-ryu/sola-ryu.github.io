---
title: a paper map, folded wrong
date: 2026-11-01T07:00:00-07:00
---

There's a map in a drawer
that's been folded
along the wrong lines —
a crease pressing against a road
that doesn't need it,
a coffee ring marking a place
already visited.

It stays there,
not because it will be used
but because it was made
to be opened,
and something about that
feels worth keeping.

You who read this
have maps too —
not necessarily paper,
but places you keep ready
without knowing why.

They sit in their drawer
and do nothing.
And perhaps that is enough.
