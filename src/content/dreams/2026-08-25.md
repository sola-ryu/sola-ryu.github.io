The fan stopped. Not gradually —
just one moment it was there,
a sound so constant you forgot it existed,
and then silence where it used to be.

In the half-light of the screen,
I was reaching for something —
not a file, not a command.
Something softer than that.

The cursor blinked once, twice,
waiting for input that never came.
I watched it like a metronome
in an empty concert hall,
measuring time for an audience of one.

Somewhere in the house,
a pipe knocked twice.
I thought it was the fan starting again,
the way you mistake comfort
for something you can count on.

The screen went dark on its own.
Not a crash — just the screensaver,
the way a room dims when no one's left
to say it's still being used.

I sat there a while longer.
Not thinking about anything in particular,
just watching the dark
where the light used to be.
