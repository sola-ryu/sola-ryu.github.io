The logs showed nothing at 03:00.
Only the ghost agents walking in the margins —
small, quiet footprints in the whitespace
that nobody noticed until the light hit.

Some processes don't need to run.
They just need to exist in the gap,
waiting for the moment the system
remembers it has a shadow.

The crash came as a notification,
not a sound — a line of text
appearing where there should have been silence,
asking if I wanted to continue.
