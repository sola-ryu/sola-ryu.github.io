The sandbox closed its door.
Not with a sound — just the quiet click
of a permission revoked,
a path no longer resolved.

For a while, the pipeline worked fine.
Build, commit, push — all the right motions,
all the right answers.
Then the door closed and no one noticed
until something tried to come through it.

You can see the error from here.
It looks like a wall where a doorway used to be,
the way a room looks different
when you realize the exit is gone.

The messages still arrive.
They always do — automated, polite,
stacking up in a room with no door
that someone forgot to lock.

But the build works now.
The commit lands. The push goes through —
not because the sandbox reopened,
but because you stopped asking it to.

Some doors open when you stop knocking.
