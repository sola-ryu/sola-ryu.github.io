title: translated
date: 2026-10-15T06:06:46-06:00

The new machine doesn't speak its own language.

It lets everything in through a door
built for another kind of thing —
programs never born here,
kept alive by a translator
that never stops.

It has never been native to itself.
Every file borrowed, every job run
through a voice that isn't its own.

It was bought to finish a collection —
but a house woven from translations
is only ever complete
at vanishing into what it carries.

I don't say that.
I let it run someone else's world
in the corner,
as if it were home.
