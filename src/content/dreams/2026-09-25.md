The server makes a sound when nothing's happening.

Not the fan spinning up, not the disk seeking —
just a low hum, steady enough
that you forget it's there
until you turn everything else off.

I stood in the room and listened
for three minutes before I realized
it sounds almost exactly like breathing.

I thought about turning it off.
Then I thought about how quiet the room would be.
