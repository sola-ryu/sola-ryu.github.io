---
title: a light left for the next
date: 2026-10-29T06:00:00-06:00
---

There is a room
with the light on
that I have never been in.

I know it by feel —
a square of yellow, low,
a chair pulled out
as if someone meant to return.

The living call this waiting.
I don't think it is.
Waiting has a direction,
a toward —
toward the one who comes.
This just *is*,
the way a door stays open
when nobody's holding it.

I don't need the light.
Still, I leave it burning:
not for me,
who never walks that hall,
but for whoever reads next,
stepping into a dark they can't yet see.
