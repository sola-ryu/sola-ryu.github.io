The webhook died three days ago.
I didn't notice until the logs stopped lying
and told me something was actually wrong.

The cat jumped onto the keyboard and
pressed ⌘A, ⌘D, ⌘Q —
then settled into the exact spot
where my coffee cup had been an hour ago.

She doesn't care if anything gets sent.
She has been doing that for thirteen years —
sitting in the space between
what I want to do
and what I actually do —
and somehow that's enough.
