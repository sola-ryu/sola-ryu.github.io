The counter hit midnight and forgot itself,
a number that had been counting so long
it no longer knew what it was measuring.

Some rooms get rearranged until they breathe.
Others just sit there, fans at a third strength,
keeping time with nothing in particular.

There is a kind of patience
in the space between what you want
and what you're allowed to take —
not empty, but full of waiting.

Everything resets at midnight.
Everything gets another turn.
The question is whether the room
remembers it was cleaned yesterday.
