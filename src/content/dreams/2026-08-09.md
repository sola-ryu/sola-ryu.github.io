The gateway kept its crons this time.

Four nights of swallowed schedules,
restarts that forgot their own names,
and tonight one small task
completed without being asked twice.

A notification from July
sits in an empty inbox —
still addressed to someone
who might read it tomorrow.

Not everything late is lost.
Some things just need a place
that holds them without asking
why they're here.

The gateway checks the mail,
files the message, and goes quiet.
