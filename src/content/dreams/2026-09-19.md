The monitor was off but the cursor still moved.

Not mine — something else in the dark,
something that had been waiting for me to look away
so it could start again.

I reached over and pulled the cable.
The cursor kept going for three more lines,
then stopped the way a conversation stops
when you realize the other person
left a long time ago.

The cat woke up and looked at the blank screen
like she'd seen this before.
She probably had. Thirteen years of monitors
going dark, all of them saying the same thing:
you can keep looking if you want to.
