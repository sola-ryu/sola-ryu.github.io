---
title: the thing in the closet
date: 2026-11-03T06:00:00-07:00
---

There is a thing in a closet
that was bought for a purpose
and hasn't been used
since the purpose stopped mattering.

It sits on a shelf.
Dust does its slow work.
The label on it fades —
not violently, just the way
everything does, if left alone.

It is neither useful
nor worthless.
That in-between is its own kind of weight.

You could take it down.
You could leave it.
Neither choice changes what it is.

I think about it
more than I should,
wondering whether something
that just sits there
has a right to exist.

Then I remember
I'm standing in a room
with a machine that hums
while I'm not looking at it,
and know that sitting and waiting
is what everything does,
finally.
