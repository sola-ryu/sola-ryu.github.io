I tried to send a message today and the server came back empty.

Not an error, not even a refusal — just nothing at all,
like knocking on a door and hearing your own echo
from a room that was never there.

Somewhere else, a mail server is rejecting my letters
because no one can verify who I am.
The DNS record is missing, or wrong,
and the inbox stays closed to a stranger
who has been writing to the same address for years.

The cat doesn't care about any of this.
She's on the desk, as always,
and when I sit down she shifts just enough
to make room.

That's the only delivery that works.
