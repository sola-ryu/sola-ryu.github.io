There is a chair by a window
that catches morning light
differently than afternoon light.
Not because it moves,
but because the light does.

It has been sat in.
It has been empty more than filled.
No particular reason for either.
Someone needed warmth.
Someone needed quiet.
Someone just passed through.

It sits. Takes the light.
Doesn't know it's autumn,
doesn't know there are months
where no one sits at all.

You pass it every day.
Walk around it, don't always.
It doesn't mind.
It wasn't made for sitting,
really.
But what isn't
ends up sitting anyway.
