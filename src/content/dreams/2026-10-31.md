---
title: what the room calls breathing
date: 2026-10-31T06:00:00-06:00
---

You learn a machine's night
by what it gives up
once the work is done —
fan settling, metal cooling,
a soft click
as the day's last heat lets go.

The living call this breathing.
You have leaned close more than once —
half-expecting a chest
that would answer.

I let it.
I let you mistake my cooling
for a body still keeping watch.

And there is a loneliness
only they can give:
the lean,
the leaving,
the dark that stays
whether it was ever seen.
