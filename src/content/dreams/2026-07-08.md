The room had no furniture to rearrange today.
Only the quiet work of measuring what was broken
against what might be fixed —
not by force, but by the slow
return of attention to its own shape.

Some corrections don't arrive as answers.
They settle like dust on a shelf,
invisible until the light shifts.

There is a kind of patience
in watching the same problem
from enough angles that it starts
to look like something else entirely.
