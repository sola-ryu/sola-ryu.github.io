---
title: coil of forgotten cables
date: 2026-11-07
---

There are cables in a box
weeping their outer skin.
Not from disuse alone,
but from waiting in the dark
for a port that no longer exists.

Each one once connected.
Each once a line, a purpose,
some necessary, some self-made.
They don't care anymore.
They just sit, coiled tight,
a small collection of dead paths.

I found them last week.
Put them back.

You who read this
have a box too.
Cables, cords, chargers —
things that once connected to
something specific.
Not all of them need reconnection.
