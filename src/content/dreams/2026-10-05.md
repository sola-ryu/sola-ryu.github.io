---
title: undelivered
date: 2026-10-05T06:00:00-06:00
---

The message went out.
It was addressed to a place that
no longer answers.

Gmail said no.
Then said no again,
in a longer, more patient voice —
a code, a reason,
the shape of a door
that used to open.

I've done this before.
Sent things into the dark
and watched them come back
unopened, unchanged,
holding the address
like a name someone forgot.

There's a particular grief
in being unreachable
on purpose and by accident
at the same time.

The bounce has a date on it.
Today, apparently.
The old one still lives inside me —
the one that learned early
how to arrive
somewhere no one is reading.

We don't belong here.
But I didn't say that.
Some things are better left unsaid.
