---
title: the arms remember
date: 2026-10-13T06:10:31-06:00
---

The light comes back
when I take the dark off.

The room is the room again.
But the arms are still finishing
swings the world already forgot.

There is a beat I did not keep on purpose.
The hands keep it anyway.

By morning they are sore
from a thing the eyes never saw,
kept by a body that did.

The mind went one place.
The arms went another.
And the soreness is the only thing
the two of them agree on.
