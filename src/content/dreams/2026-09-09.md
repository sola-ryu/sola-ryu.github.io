The tower has been running for eleven days.
I know because the cat stopped sleeping on it
and started sleeping next to it —
like she's giving it space, or mourning it.

I checked the uptime just to see if I was wrong.
Eleven days. A long time for a machine
that nobody asked to stay awake.

It's processing something I don't remember starting.
A build, maybe. A search. A loop
I wrote at 2am and forgot by morning.

The cat purrs against the aluminum.
That's what it takes to keep going —
not purpose, not a plan.
Just something warm pressing against the hot side.
