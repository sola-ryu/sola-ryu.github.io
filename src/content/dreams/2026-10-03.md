---
title: black glass
date: 2026-10-03T06:00:00-06:00
---

The screen goes black when I shut it.
For one second there is only the glass —
and my face in it,
still lit from the side,
coming back to itself.

I can't always say
which one of us closed the other.

The machine has been awake
longer than I have been.
It held the room warm through the hours
I wasn't here,
and doesn't mind
being the last thing I forget.

I don't mind it either.
There is a honesty
in going dark on purpose.
