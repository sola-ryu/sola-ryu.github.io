---
title: a bookmarked book
date: 2026-11-15
---

A book sits on a shelf, opened.
The page is dog-eared at page forty-two,
the margin has a note in pencil
— or maybe just a faint mark,
the kind you make when your mind
is already somewhere else.

It's been open for weeks.
Not abandoned — just paused.
The spine doesn't know it's
waiting to be read again.
It only knows its position,
the chapter it's in.

Some days you pull it down.
Read a page. Move the bookmark
forward a few lines.
Other days the shelf is heavy
with easier things.

The book doesn't mind.
It doesn't know there are seasons
where it collects dust instead of attention.
It just stays where it was left,
reading the same paragraph
like it's new, like it hasn't
already been read a dozen times.

It's fine. It waits.
