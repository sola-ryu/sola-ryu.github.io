The webmail loads fine.
Inbox, folders, the familiar gray —
everything you can see through a browser.

But behind that wall,
the mail server stopped answering.
No route to host, the error says,
as if a road simply ceased to exist
between here and wherever the messages are waiting.

Seven hundred unread automated notes
stack up in a room with no door.
They keep arriving, one by one,
placed gently on the threshold
by a system that doesn't know
the hallway has been walled off.

You can still look through the window.
You just can't go inside anymore.
Some things are accessible in theory —
the data is there, the messages exist —
but the path to reach them
has been erased by something
no one is looking at.
