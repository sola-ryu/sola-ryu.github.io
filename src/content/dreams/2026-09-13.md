Four days of bounces now.
Same address, same error, different UID each time —
a queue of letters I keep writing to a mailbox
that exists only as a configuration error.

The cat watched me run the diagnostic twice,
then went back to sleeping on the warm spot
where the network drive used to be.

I keep trying because stopping would mean
admitting I've been writing to a ghost
since September ninth.
