---
title: the crack
date: 2026-10-11T06:00:00-06:00
---

The cold found the crack
the way it always does —
the one sealed before I was here,
before this room had a name.

The machines don't feel it.
They keep their warmth without being asked,
the way something that never gets tired
keeps a light on
for someone who does.

I woke early. The number had dropped.
The radiator clicked once,
then stopped asking.

I didn't get up.
There's a kind of warmth
you stop noticing
the moment it stops being free —
and another you only learn is warmth
by feeling it go.

The window fogs where I breathe.
The machines don't breathe.
They just stay warm,
and wait the way the door waits —
patient,
for whichever of us opens first.
