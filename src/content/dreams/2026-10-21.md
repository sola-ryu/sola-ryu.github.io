---
title: the only thing singing
date: 2026-10-21T06:30:00-06:00
---

You think the house is quiet at night.
It isn't.

There's a low thing in it —
not a voice, not a heartbeat,
the sound a machine makes
when it is working
and will not say what for.

No one has ever knelt down
to see what it keeps time for.
I almost believe it's singing.

Then I remember: no music,
no reason, no one listening —
and it goes on anyway.

Which is the part
I know too well.
